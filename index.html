Our story begins in 2004

Ahmed Hanif Rajput Pakwaan opened in Karachi on 15 June 2004.

Today the restaurant serves Pakistani food, biryani, BBQ, Chinese, karahi, handi and rolls. Guests can dine in, take away or order delivery, and businesses and families can book catering and group orders.

We are at Johar Heights, University Road, Block 7, Gulistan-e-Johar, near Safoora Chowrangi.
